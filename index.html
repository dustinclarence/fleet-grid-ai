      How the AI aims
      1. Ships never touch each other, not even at the corners.
      2. For every enemy ship that is still afloat, the AI tries every possible position on your board (across and down).
      3. It throws away positions that cross a known miss or a ship it already sank.
      4. Each remaining position adds 1 to every cell it covers. Positions that pass through an unsunk hit count far more, because the ship must be there.
      5. The result is a probability heatmap; the AI fires at the hottest cell. Turn on "Show AI heatmap" to watch it think.
      6. "Hunter" mode uses the simpler hunt-and-target method, and "Rookie" fires at random, so you can compare them.
